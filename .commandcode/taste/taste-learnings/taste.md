# Taste Learnings
- Prefers scoped, non-breaking changes: isolate new behavior to the relevant mode/module (e.g., Practice Mode only) and leave existing logic (Standard/Exam mode, scoring/calculation) untouched. Confidence: 0.8
- Prefers state updates to happen immediately at the point of user interaction (e.g., update score/status records as soon as an option is selected), rather than deferring to submission. Confidence: 0.7
- Prefers a proper multiselect dropdown component (e.g., Select2-style dropdown with checkboxes/chips) over native `<select multiple>` list boxes for multi-select inputs. Confidence: 0.8
- Prefers compact, visually consistent UI controls; dislikes elements that look oversized or visually out of place, and wants new components to match the sizing/radius/font of existing form controls. Confidence: 0.7
- Prefers detailed, educational explanations that teach the full concept and related knowledge behind each question, rather than a brief restatement of the correct answer. Confidence: 0.8
- Wants study content written with domain expertise and localized language: Hindi-language questions explained in Hindi (keeping English technical terms as-is), computer questions answered accurately. Confidence: 0.7
- Prefers completing remaining/leftover work in a single consolidated pass ("in one go") rather than many small incremental batches. Confidence: 0.6
- For large, repetitive content-editing/generation jobs, prefers working in substantially larger batches (~100 items at a time) to finish faster, rather than small ~20-item batches. Confidence: 0.9
- Prefers concise, direct answers to direct questions (e.g., a simple yes/no status confirmation) rather than lengthy status summaries or unsolicited offers of additional follow-up work. Confidence: 0.6
